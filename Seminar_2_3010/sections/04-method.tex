\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

% [27/10] Thêm câu định hướng: mục này chỉ mô tả phần mapping (RQ1 đến RQ3); thiết kế pilot ở mục Pilot, RQ5 ở Discussion.
\new{This section describes how we built and coded the map that answers RQ1 to RQ3. The pilot for RQ4 has its own design (Section~\ref{sec:pilot}), and RQ5 draws on both (Section~\ref{sec:discussion}).} We followed the guidelines for systematic mapping studies in software engineering~\cite{petersen}. The protocol, search log, codebook and all decisions are in the replication package.

\subsection{Scope}
\label{sec:scope}

% [27/10] Phạm vi viết theo đúng tập đã quét (scanned_papers.csv): mọi track, kể cả DS, SRC, và bài chỉ có trong main hoặc
% Companion volume (có bài workshop đăng trong Companion, như C202); chỉ loại sự kiện có kỷ yếu riêng, nên "main proceedings"
% thêm vế đối lập với các sự kiện đó. Câu lý do bỏ "where legacy settings without tests appear" (là kết quả, không phải lý do).
A paper is in scope if it appears in the 2024, 2025 or 2026 edition of ICSE, FSE or ASE: \new{in any track of the conference program, including the poster, doctoral symposium and student research competition tracks; in the conference's main or Companion volume; or, for journal-first papers, as a presentation at the conference.} We call this set the main proceedings\new{, as opposed to co-located events with proceedings of their own}. 
%\new{ ICSE, FSE and ASE are the flagship venues of software engineering, so their papers show which evaluation practices reviewers accept. Their industry tracks also report migrations of industrial and legacy systems. Co-located workshops and conferences that publish their own proceedings}
\new{ICSE, FSE and ASE were selected as major general software engineering conferences with both research and industry tracks.\cmtpink{P30, Major; R1, EIC, DA}{This does not explain why ISSTA, the flagship testing venue where oracle work concentrates, is excluded in 2024 and 2026, when it was not co-located, or why TSE, TOSEM and EMSE count only as journal-first. Venue choice can bias exactly the RQ1 shares the paper stresses (formal 4\%, differential 29\%). Scan ISSTA 2024--2026 with the frozen string as a sensitivity set, or give a substantive reason and scope the title and general statements to the three venues.} We examine the evaluation practices reported in their peer-reviewed papers, including studies of industrial and legacy-system migrations. Co-located workshops and conferences that publish their own proceedings} (ReCode, LLM4Code, FORGE, AGENT, AIware, AISM, ICPC, MSR and others) are out of scope: the set of events changes from year to year, so counting them would make years incomparable.\cmtpink{P31, Minor; R1; open since R10}{Workshops that publish in the Companion volume also change from year to year (Companion-only papers: FSE 4, 74 and 95 in 2024--2026; ASE none), so the same argument applies to them, and they sit in the yearly denominators of Section 5.1. Exclude them and recompute, or justify keeping them and report their number per year.} ISSTA~2025 and Internetware~2025, which were co-hosted with FSE that year only, are excluded for the same reason. \new{Related papers outside the corpus} are cited in Sections~\ref{sec:intro}, \new{\ref{sec:related}} and~\ref{sec:discussion} but not counted.


\subsection{Search}
\label{sec:search}

% [27/10] "the LLM criterion" viết lại theo I1 (neural model hoặc LLM). Thêm 216 bài chỉ có trên Crossref (13 hit, đều bị
% loại; đếm từ scanned_papers.csv) để khớp với phạm vi. Bỏ ICSE 2025 khỏi các track poster "không có danh sách": trang
% track ghi rõ ICSE 2025 không nhận poster riêng, poster chỉ đến từ bài đã được nhận ở track khác, nên không có gì bị sót.
% Mã I1 và I3 chưa được định nghĩa ở đây (Bảng tiêu chí ở mục 4.3), nên câu và chú thích Bảng 2 trỏ tới mục 4.3 thay vì
% dùng mã trần.
We applied one frozen keyword string to titles and abstracts:
\begin{center}
\fbox{\parbox{0.94\columnwidth}{\small\ttfamily translat* OR transpil* OR migrat* OR moderni* OR legacy OR porting OR ported OR upgrad* OR deprecat* OR \textquotedbl cross-language\textquotedbl{} OR \textquotedbl cross-lingual\textquotedbl{} OR equivalen*}}
\end{center}
The string omits ``oracle'' and ``behavior*'', \new{because they} pulled in unrelated testing papers, and omits ``LLM'', because many neural papers do not use the word; \new{whether a paper uses a neural model or an LLM is checked at screening (Section~\ref{sec:selection}).} Paper lists came from each conference program on conf.researchr.org, covering every track \new{that had a published paper list (the FSE~2026 and ASE~2026 poster tracks did not; Section~\ref{sec:threats}). We cross-checked them against the main and Companion volumes of each conference through Crossref, which added 216 papers that appear in no track program. Some of them come from workshops that publish in the Companion volume, and none of their 13 keyword hits was included.} Missing abstracts were taken from Semantic Scholar and arXiv. \new{The final scan, on October 23, 2026,} covered 4,451 main-proceedings papers and returned 430 keyword hits; \new{for 80 papers without an available abstract, the string was matched against the title only}.\cmtpink{P32, Minor; R1}{Publisher pages (ACM DL, IEEE Xplore) normally have these abstracts. Fetch the 80, rerun the string and screen any new hits; this removes the limitation instead of discussing it, including its effect on the 2024 share in Section 5.1.}

\subsection{Selection}
\label{sec:selection}

% [27/10] Thêm lý do cho từng tiêu chí I (I3 là tầng riêng, báo ở 5.4); E4 viết thành quy tắc vì không bài nào bị loại theo E4.
% Chín luật L tách thành Bảng 4 (trước chỉ là văn xuôi) để mã được định nghĩa trước khi dùng; cột N đếm trên 431 candidate (cột clarification_rule).
% Mỗi luật ghi rõ tiêu chí nó áp vào (L-TEST vẫn cần LLM; L-TASK áp cả I2 vì có benchmark); bỏ "recurring" vì L-DATA chưa dùng.
% I2: "uses" thành "studies" cho khớp cách đã áp: bài chỉ dùng oracle chấm công cụ của mình (bộ dịch rule-based, ModelX) bị loại;
% 4 bài chỉ đạt I2 (RustRepoTrans, PROGnosticator, MBTA, FreshBrew) đều đề xuất hoặc nghiên cứu cách đánh giá.
A paper is included if it satisfies I4 and at least one of I1--I3, \new{provided that no exclusion criterion applies} (Table~\ref{tab:criteria}). \new{The criteria follow from the RQs. I1 admits papers that use a neural model or an LLM to migrate code and say how equivalence is checked, even if only by BLEU, so that every included paper has an oracle to code.\cmtpink{P34, Minor; R2; open since R12}{The title says LLM-based, but I1 admits any neural model: MBTA evaluates TransCoder and j2py (rule-based) with no LLM, yet its LLM role is E (it should be N), and the BatFix row of Figure 3 and the UniTrans and TransAgent-baseline rows of Table 9 show TransCoder outputs. Define LLM inclusively in Section 2 or adjust the title, and report how many papers evaluate only pre-LLM models.} I2 adds papers on benchmarks and evaluation methods, since these define oracles even when no translator is proposed. I3 covers papers on detecting whether two programs in different languages are equivalent. Their detectors could serve as oracles, but the papers evaluate the detectors rather than migrations, so they form a separate tier, which the results report apart from the main corpus (Section~\ref{sec:i3}). Secondary studies (E4) belong in related work (Section~\ref{sec:related}), not in the map.}

\subsection{Classification and extraction}
\label{sec:extraction}

% [27/10] Ba đoạn: trích gì (hai bộ phân loại, bảy trường, bằng chứng ở cột evidence của extraction.csv), mã thế nào (quy tắc
% chính và bảy quy tắc ranh giới theo comment 7, 10, 11 và V2 (phương án B) của Hà Cao; ví dụ test dịch kèm code: AlphaTrans), phân tích ra sao
% (trường nào trả lời RQ nào). Bớt lặp "come from", "benchmark", "We code".
\new{We code each paper with the two classification schemes of Section~\ref{sec:background}. The migration kinds correspond one to one to the source and target types that criterion I1 lists (Table~\ref{tab:criteria}); every included paper has at least one. The nine oracle categories began as a scheme that we drafted during protocol development from the papers we knew before the search, and we refined it during extraction. For example, formal verification was separated from property-based testing, and the test-based categories were redefined by the origin of their expected outputs.} For each included paper we \new{drafted values for the seven fields of Table~\ref{tab:form} from the full text and recorded short quotations, with their locations, as evidence. Field~5 splits the four test-based categories into the subtypes listed in the table; for differential testing it records the source of the inputs, because the expected outputs are always those of the original.}

\new{We then applied the following rules to turn the drafts into final codes. Only oracles that judge the final output count; one} used solely inside the technique, such as a compiler in a repair loop or a filter on training data, \new{does not.} \new{Seven further rules handle boundary cases. The paper must report the oracle's result on the evaluated outputs. Compilation or a syntax check counts as O-b whenever the paper reports how many outputs pass it, even if only as a breakdown of failures by stage or in a figure; compilation implied only by passing tests is not coded.\cmtpink{P40, Minor; R2, R1}{Two identical evaluations can be coded differently (one prints a failure breakdown, the other does not), and a compile count in a breakdown does not decide whether the migrated program behaves like the original (your definition of an oracle). Report compile rates used as a success metric separately, and consider a strongest-oracle-per-paper view (no executing oracle / tests / differential / formal) that answers RQ1 directly.} If a system translates tests along with the code, checks that touch only the translated tests, such as compiling them, are not oracles of the translation. In test migration, where the migrated test is itself the output, running it on the target counts as O-d. Tests translated by hand when a benchmark was built are benchmark tests (O-c), whereas tests that a paper's authors translate for their own evaluation are translated tests (O-d).\cmtpink{P41, Minor; R2}{The O-c/O-d boundary follows timing, not origin: hand-translated tests are O-c if a benchmark builder translated them and O-d if the study's authors did, and HumanEval/131 shows that O-c tests share O-d's failure mode. O-d also merges tests translated by the system under evaluation (not independent) with tests translated by hand (independent). Add independence (pre-existing, built by the authors, produced by the system) as an explicit axis; G4 needs exactly that.} LLM-written tests whose expected outputs have no stated source are coded O-e, since nothing shows that they were checked against the original. The role of the LLM follows what the LLM does in the study, whatever kind of technique is proposed; a non-LLM technique compared with LLM baselines is coded as baseline only. We also re-checked every} number in the disagreement analysis (Section~\ref{sec:rq3}) against the paper.

% [28/10] RQ3 thêm câu nguồn của đoạn người chấm (cờ reports_rater_agreement, ngoài form). Câu Appendix tách riêng vì không thuộc RQ3.
\new{The analysis counts papers. Section~\ref{sec:overview} describes the corpus with fields~1 to~3, and RQ1 counts the oracles of field~4 (Section~\ref{sec:rq1}). RQ2 reports the origins of field~5 and compares oracle use across four contexts: a legacy source or not, the migration kind, the track and the publication year (Section~\ref{sec:rq2}). RQ3 draws on fields~6 and~7. For the papers with human evaluation, we also record whether they report agreement between raters (Section~\ref{sec:rq3}). Appendix~\ref{app:primary} gives the codes for every paper. We did not score the quality of the included papers: the RQs ask how they evaluate equivalence, and RQ3 covers the weaknesses that they state or show.}
